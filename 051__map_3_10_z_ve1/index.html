Building 

On your street you can buy parcels, erect or level buildings. Buildings are erected for a certain time. For levelling a building you get 50% of what you have invested in the building.

Buildings now have life. You have to keep an eye on it, because if it becomes null the building falls.  

Crimes 

You can commit lots of crimes on your or other player's street (no matter) and win cash and gain risk.

When you're on your street you can supply the factories with cash and get their production. You can (and you must) gather the turnover of your bars.

Accountant - If you don't feel like gathering the turnover and the production, there is the Extra Accountant. It takes 50% of the turnover and the production every game day.

When you're on other player's street you can rob factories and bars. Up on the left there is a link Beat the Bastard. You can commit crimes on other's map only once a day (beat them or rob their buildings). Collapse help
