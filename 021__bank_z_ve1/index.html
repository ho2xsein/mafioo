The Bank has limited number of transactions. Every time you draw money or deposit it costs you 1 transaction. Out of it you can't neither deposit, nor draw cash. Every day you receive 2 more transactions free.

Deposits of cash

  • In the bank you can deposit cash and accumulate interest on it. The more it stays the more interest it accumulates. The accumulation of interest is every game day.
  • When your cash is in the bank they can't be taken from you in a fight!

Taxes

  • In the bank you can pay your taxes.
  • Taxes are calculated as for base is taken the growth of your cash every day. If the growth is positive the tax percentage is 5%, if it's negative is 4%.
  • If you have many unpaid taxes they can nail you.
  • If you owe more than permissible taxes – there's a possibility to be caught by the cops.

Loans

You can draw loans from the bank by putting strength and intellect points as stake. If you can't repay your debt in time (including interest) the money lender would send his guards to take his money from you. If you don't have the needed cash in time the guards will beat you and you will lose the strength and intellect you have put as a stake.
  • You need at least 5000 strength and 5000 intellect
  • You can't put as stake more than 10% of your strength and intellect
  • 1 point strength and 1 point intellect as a stake is equal to 10$
  • The maximum sum you can draw is 500 000$

Exchange

You can get money for your credits there.
  • The exchange rate depends on deals on the black market from the last few days and changes every day.
