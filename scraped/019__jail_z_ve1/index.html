You get in the Jail if your Risk  is too high and a cop nails you. When you're in the jail you can't fight with anybody, neither to take part in the crimes of your gang. It's really fucking shit if you're the boss.

While you're in the jail, you can play in The Casino. You can go to the Fitness or attend courses. You can go to The Bank, look at the rankings or write letters. You can even chat with other jail-birds – it's cute, yeah?

But how can I get out of the jail?

You can do this by credit (paid lawyer). Also you can try to get at the guards by cash (jailbreaking). The lawyer's price is one or more credits, it depends on your criminal record.

Everybody can see that you're a jail-bird from the icon jail-bird.

Revolt

If you don't have cash or credits to get out of the jail, you can rise in revolt. If you organize revolt and at least 30% of jail-birds, who will take part in it rise, you'll succeed to get out of it. Otherwise remaining time of every rebel's sentence will double.

Tournament of Armwrestling

Take part in a tournament of armwrestling! Only Strength weighs. The first five win the gathered cash, the first three of them win credits . If you get out of the jail before the competition, you drop out as a competitor!!!

Get a friend off

You can get a friend or a fellow of your gang if you hire a paid lawyer for the jail-bird. The lawyer's price is one or more credits, it depends on jail-bird's criminal record.

  • 1 — criminal record till 100
  • 2 — criminal record from 101 to 200
  • 3 — criminal record from 201 to 300
